The source page explicitly labels this individual sound Public Domain. It is
bundled for offline use; OpenYI does not contact the source site at runtime.
The author is credited voluntarily. This sound is separate from the repository's
MIT-licensed program code. The source-page license declaration, rather than a
general assumption about the website, is the basis for redistribution.

At runtime FFmpeg downmixes/resamples the sound to mono 16 kHz, applies loudness
normalization (-9 LUFS target, -1 dBTP ceiling, LRA 7), and encodes AAC-LC at
32 kbit/s. Computer playback decodes the same prepared AAC to signed 16-bit PCM.
